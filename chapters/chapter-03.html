Part Two — Obstacle One: Position · Chapter 03

Why Position Is an Obstacle

Position determines the order in which players act. Acting later usually provides information that players acting earlier do not yet have. Acting first means making decisions while opponents’ intentions remain unknown. That information gap can turn an otherwise playable hand into a difficult one.

03♠♦♣♥POSITION · PRESSURE · HAND STRENGTH · RISK

The challenge

Position is the order in which players act during a betting round. Acting later usually provides an informational advantage: you have seen more of your opponents’ choices before deciding what to do. Acting early means making a decision while several players still have the opportunity to respond.

Look beyond the obvious

This difference affects more than the first decision before the flop. On later streets, a player who acts first may have to make a choice without knowing whether opponents will check, bet, or raise. A player acting last can often respond to the action already taken and may control whether the pot grows.

A more disciplined approach

Position does not make a weak hand strong or guarantee a winning result. It changes how much information you have and how comfortably you can realize the potential of a hand. Hands that are manageable late in the order can become difficult when played from early position, especially when several opponents remain.

Put the idea into practice

Identify your seat before evaluating your cards. Ask who acts after you and how that could complicate the hand if you enter the pot.

Chapter takeaway
Identify the obstacle, evaluate the situation with the information available, and choose a deliberate response rather than reacting automatically.
